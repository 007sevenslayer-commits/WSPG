Understanding Social Audit at WSPG

A Social Audit is an official process through which Women Security Pressure Group (WSPG) measures, understands, reports, and improves its social performance and impact. It ensures complete accountability to the communities we serve, our donors, stakeholders, and government bodies.

Through public social audit meetings, program evaluations, and financial disclosures, WSPG reviews resource allocation, project outcomes, and beneficiary feedback to maintain the highest standards of integrity and social responsibility.

Our Commitments

Pillars of Our Social Audit

Transparency

Open sharing of financial statements, annual budgets, and project expenditures with the public.

Community Feedback

Direct engagement with community members and victims to evaluate real-world project impact.

Impact Evaluation

Measuring quantitative and qualitative outcomes of our anti-violence campaigns and advocacy.

Good Governance

Adhering strictly to national NGO regulations, institutional ethics, and stakeholder standards.
