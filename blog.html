Our Activities

Recent Initiatives & Advocacy Programs

Discover key programs and advocacy efforts led by the Women Security Pressure Group (WSPG) to advance women's leadership, voter education, legal protection, and psychosocial support.

Reporting Period • Federal / Nepal

Lobbying with Major Political Parties for Women's Leadership

Supported by KFB/DKA, Vienna, Austria

WSPG conducted 10 advocacy meetings with major political parties, including the Rastriya Swatantra Party (RSP), advocating for women's political leadership and participation in federal elections through a 20-point Memorandum.

The 20-point Memorandum urged party leaders to ensure at least 50% women representation in direct elections, increase female leadership in candidate selection committees, support marginalized women, and prevent perpetrators of violence against women from receiving election nominations. It also called for eliminating discrimination and harassment, guaranteeing constitutional rights, and allocating funding for female candidates.

While all political parties responded positively, the Rastriya Swatantra Party (RSP) expressed an explicit commitment to implement the recommendations. WSPG will regularly monitor progress on these political commitments.

Community Program • Kathmandu Metropolitan City

Voter Education & Mock Elections

Organized across Wards in KMC

Three grassroots voter education programs were conducted in Kathmandu Metropolitan City to foster inclusive and gender-sensitive democratic participation.

The sessions educated community members on election rights, secret voting, ballot validity, and election day procedures. Dedicated support was provided for senior citizens and persons with disabilities to ensure accessible voting experiences.

A hands-on mock election allowed participants to practice casting confidential votes using the Swastika stamp across Proportional Representation and First-Past-The-Post ballots, building overall confidence for voting independently.

2-Day Training • KMC Ward No. 30

Legal Awareness on Violence Against Youth & Women

Supported by KMC Ward No. 30

A two-day legal awareness program engaged 40 women and youth, focusing on legal remedies and prevention strategies against violence.

Facilitated by Prof. Dr. Shashi Adhikari, the training explored domestic violence laws, legal consequences for perpetrators, and early intervention techniques. The program was chaired by Irada Gautam (Chairperson), facilitated by Radha Bista, and concluded by Geeta Bhattarai (Member).

Outcome: Participants enhanced their legal knowledge, recognized remedies for violence, and gained confidence to support survivors within their communities.

Interactive Workshop • KMC Ward No. 30

Psychosocial Counseling & Mental Health Awareness

Coordinated by WSPG Secretariat

Focused on mental health literacy, stress management, and supportive communication for 40 community members, youth, and survivors.

Facilitated by Antina Ranjitkar (Psychosocial Counselor, Nagarik Aawaz), the session covered early identification of stress, anxiety, and depression, utilizing interactive group exercises and Mandala art therapy. The session was chaired by Baijayanti Karki (General Secretary) and coordinated by Buddhikala Khanal (Executive Director).

Outcome: Participants acquired practical stress-management tools, embraced non-judgmental communication, and learned to support individuals facing emotional distress.
