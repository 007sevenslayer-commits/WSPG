History of Women Security Pressure Group

Women Security Pressure Group (WSPG) was established in 1991 by a group of women’s rights activists. Joining together from backgrounds that included working for women’s rights and development, they unified their mission to promote the rights of Nepali women.

The idea was to create a common forum for all women and women’s organizations with no political affiliation, so as to liberate the organization from any constraints. Since the day of its establishment WSPG has been working against all forms of discrimination, exploitation and injustice towards women.

Working as advocates, they have pressured the government to change discriminatory laws and form new laws that favour women. A true non-profit organization, WSPG is run by volunteers who form an executive committee and advisory board to oversee their continued progress.

With the head office centrally located in Kathmandu, WSPG also encompasses 35 district branches and 58 network organizations throughout Nepal. WSPG continues to fight for further improvements in women’s rights, demanding an end to violence against women and the equal representation of women in the policy-making levels of government.
