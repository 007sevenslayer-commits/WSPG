Collective Voice

Raising Voices Against Violence

Since its inception, the Women Security Pressure Group has been raising its voice on violence against women and taking effective steps to prevent and reduce it. The organization was born out of advocacy around the rape case of a six-year-old girl nearly three decades ago.

Along with protecting and promoting women's rights and participation, WSPG actively works to reduce gender violence and provide justice and relief to victims while pushing to hold culprits accountable. Women Security Pressure Group publishes news, research, and reports in its flagship publication, Collective Voice (Samuhik Aawaj). To date, 18 episodes of Collective Voice have been published.
